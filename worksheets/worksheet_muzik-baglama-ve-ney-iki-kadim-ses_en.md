While one vibrates tangible matter via manual strumming, the other transmutes empty hollow space into resonance through breath from human lungs.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
Traditional Turkish music diverges radically from the twelve-tone equal temperament system dominant in Western classical music. A whole tone interval is subdivided into nine microtonal commas, empowering performers to mirror the most elusive inflections of the human voice.

Movable nylon frets along the neck of the bağlama allow instantaneous recalibration of microtonal modes according to regional idioms. On the ney, lacking rigid frets entirely, microtones sculpted through embouchure angles, breath pressure, and head tilts represent the apex of modal mastery.

At first glance, these two voices seem native to contrasting worlds. The bağlama likewise stands as the sanctuary for migrants, expatriates, and star-crossed lovers. One speaks via breath, the other through a plectrum; yet both articulate the millennia-old beating heart of Anatolia.
