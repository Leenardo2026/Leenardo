Working in a contemporary office setting, Ayşe struggled for a long period with the mental exhaustion caused by repeatedly snoozing her alarm. Rushing out of the house in the morning led to her energy being depleted before even arriving at work, fostering a reactive mindset all day.

Indeed, waking up early ceased to be a burden for Ayşe and became the most tangible testament to self-respect and quality of life.

Struggling to hear her own voice amid the city's clamor and ceaseless professional demands, Ayşe knew full well that the true turning point in her life had begun with a modest morning resolution. In former days, mornings spent hurriedly silencing her alarm and rushing into streets had caused her to be depleted at the day's outset and grow estranged from herself.

For Ayşe, these tranquil morning hours are not about getting lost in daily bustle, but the freedom to commence each new day as the conscious author of her own story.
