### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
Turkish coffee is one of the world's oldest and most unique brewing methods. Finely ground coffee, water, and sugar to taste are placed in a small pot called cezve.

It is brewed slowly on low heat, producing rich velvety foam on top. The coffee is served in small cups accompanied by a glass of water and Turkish delight.

There is a famous Turkish proverb: 'A single cup of coffee carries forty years of gratitude.' This drink symbolizes profound friendship, respect, and heartfelt conversation.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
Against modern coffee habits consumed on the run in disposable paper cups amid the day's dizzying rush, Turkish coffee embodies a radically different rhythm of life. It is the very synonym for unhurried deceleration and warm companionship. With velvety crema rising over gentle embers, fine porcelain cups, and a sip of cold water on the side, coffee in Turkey is not merely a beverage, but a venerable ritual sealing friendship. The proverb 'A single cup of coffee carries forty years of gratitude' encapsulates the enduring mutual respect and loyalty underpinning its flavor.

Once the coffee is savored to the bottom, the second chapter of the conversation unfolds. Inverting the cup onto the saucer to cool opens the gateway to an intimate rite of curiosity and camaraderie. Reading patterns in the grounds dried at the bottom is less a claim to divination than an elegant pretext for friends to unburden their hearts and voice cherished hopes. Turkish coffee stands as a warm, patient sanctuary brewed against a world consuming life in haste.
