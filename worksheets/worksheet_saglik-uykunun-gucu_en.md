> Ali visiting the doctor and changing his sleep habits recounted in past tense.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)

Dr. Kaya examined him and said that sleep is the best medicine. Ali listened to this advice and removed the television from his bedroom.

A few days later he felt more refreshed and focused easily on his work.

## 📌 SEVİYE B1: Ali's Sleep Discipline and Mental Peace

**Dil Bilgisi Odağı:** Conjunctions (-dığı için, -erek), Necessity (-meli), Adverbial Participles
> Ali stepping away from evening screens and gaining mental calm told in flowing intermediate prose.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
Because of a heavy work pace, Ali spent his evenings staring at screens, which made falling asleep difficult. When he spoke with Dr. Kaya, he learned that blue light disrupts melatonin balance and keeps the brain alert.

Following this, Ali set a firm rule to turn off all digital devices after nine in the evening. Before going to bed, he began preparing his body for rest by drinking chamomile tea and listening to soft music.

Within a few weeks, he began waking up much more refreshed, and his focus at work improved noticeably.

### 📚 Kilit Kelimeler (Key Vocabulary)
- **blue light**: blue light
- **melatonin**: melatonin
- **digital device**: digital device
- **increase in focus**: increase in focus

### ❓ Anlama ve Konuşma Soruları (Comprehension Questions)
1. According to Dr. Kaya, how does blue light affect the body?
   *(Cevap: It disrupts melatonin balance and prevents the brain from resting at night.)*

## 📌 SEVİYE B2: Ali's Sleep Rhythm and Renewal Process

**Dil Bilgisi Odağı:** Reported Speech, Compound Sentences, Descriptive Phrases
> Ali breaking his cycle of exhaustion and transforming his bedroom into a serene sanctuary told with descriptive depth.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
For long months, Ali woke up with a persistent feeling of burnout, trying to stay on his feet with strong coffees throughout the day. In his lengthy conversation with Dr. Kaya, he realized that sleep is not merely staying still, but a session in which the mind cleanses the day's residue.

Guided by this advice, Ali purged his bedroom of all electronic devices, turning it into a dim and cool sanctuary. As he fell into deeper sleep at night, he began opening his eyes feeling vigorous and light, even before the alarm rang in the morning.

He now views guarding evening serenity as his most valuable source of health and daily productivity.

## 📌 SEVİYE C1: Rebirth in the Stillness of Night: Ali's Sleep Journey

**Dil Bilgisi Odağı:** Literary Narrative, Advanced Participle Clauses, Nuanced Mood Depiction
> Ali stepping away from modern hectic life to rediscover nocturnal peace under Dr. Kaya's guidance, written in elegant, natural Turkish.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
Ali had knocked on Dr. Kaya's door to leave behind those days when, after nights buried in the cold glow of screens, he awoke every morning with a mind heavy as lead. The seasoned physician had described sleep not as an ordinary pause, but as a sheltered harbor where the soul and cells seek refuge after a tempestuous day.

Taking this counsel as his guide, Ali purged his bedroom of notification chimes and artificial lighting, as if transforming it into a peaceful sanctuary. As evening darkness fell, sitting by the window and turning book pages alongside a cup of warm sage tea smoothed away the waves of thoughts accumulated throughout the day.

Now, awakening at every dawn with the first light filtering through the window, he embraces life anew with the unmistakable clarity bestowed by restorative sleep.

### 📚 Kilit Kelimeler (Key Vocabulary)
- **sheltered harbor**: sheltered harbor
- **heavy as lead**: heavy as lead
- **to settle / calm down**: to settle / calm down
- **clarity / lucidity**: clarity / lucidity

### ❓ Anlama ve Konuşma Soruları (Comprehension Questions)

   *(Cevap: As a sheltered harbor where the soul and cells take refuge after a tempestuous day.)*
