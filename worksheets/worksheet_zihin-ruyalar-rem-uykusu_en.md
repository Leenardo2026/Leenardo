### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
When we close our eyes and drift to sleep, we assume our consciousness falls completely silent. Yet contemporary neuroscience reveals that our brain conducts just as feverish an enterprise at night as during daytime. Particularly during the phase called REM sleep, rapid movements initiate behind closed eyelids and our heart rate accelerates. At this stage, the brain expends an energy level rivaling wakefulness. Sleep is no mere halt; it is an intensive mental workshop where memory is sifted, trivial details purged, and fresh creative connections forged.

The most striking hallmark of dreams is the temporary suspension of our logic filter during these hours. When the frontal brain regions responsible for critical reasoning withdraw into slumber, emotional centers seize control. Thus in our dreams we defy gravity, meet familiar faces in absurd locales, and embrace the bizarre contradictions without surprise. Experts consider this process a vital emotional safety valve that resolves the day's complex tensions within a safe simulation environment. In short, dreams enable our mind to inaugurate the following day with a pristine clean slate.
