## 📌 SEVİYE A1: The Big Plane Tree

**Dil Bilgisi Odağı:** Present Simple, There is / are, Adjective Phrases
> The old plane tree in the square, birds, and playing children described with simple adjectives.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
In our neighborhood square, there is a very big and old plane tree. The green leaves of the tree give wonderful shade in summer.

Birds sing cheerfully in high branches and build nests. Children gather under the tree after school and play games.

Everyone in the neighborhood loves this beautiful tree and protects it.

## 📌 SEVİYE B2: Witness of Time: The Neighborhood's Living Plane Tree

**Dil Bilgisi Odağı:** Compound Sentences, Literary Imagery, Descriptive Nuance
> The ancient tree bridging generations and standing as a sanctuary against urban expansion described with rich nuance.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
As surrounding streets widened and wooden mansions gave way to multistory concrete buildings, the grand plane tree clung its roots even tighter to the soil, as if defying time. Each deep fissure on its trunk carries the traces of joyful weddings, wistful farewells, and childhood laughter belonging to the neighborhood's past.

Fresh buds appearing on its branches in spring offer a safe refuge to hundreds of migratory birds each year, turning the square into a natural orchestra. Sitting under the shade of this deeply rooted presence, the residents forget the haste of modern life and taste the peace of reconnecting with nature.

## 📌 SEVİYE C1: The Silent Memory of Roots: Story of the Century-Old Plane Tree

**Dil Bilgisi Odağı:** Literary Style, Advanced Relative and Adverbial Clauses, Poetic Prose
> The plane tree standing as the neighborhood's living memory against time, depicted in graceful, authentic literary Turkish.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
As the tumult of the city overtakes the streets, the century-old plane tree standing tall in the center of the square is the guardian spirit of the neighborhood, not only with branches reaching for the sky, but with a silent memory anchored deep in the earth. Having witnessed for generations the first steps of toddlers on these cobblestones, the whispers of lovers, and the wistful musings of the elderly, its craggy bark is like a living neighborhood chronicle.

Despite the suffocating pressure of concrete construction, this grand presence—cherished by the collective care of the residents—offers an emerald dome in summer heat that strips away human hurriedness and invites one into the tranquility of the earth. When the evening wind rustles its leaves, it is as though all the whispers of bygone days take flight at once, imparting an indescribable serenity to the hearts of those who listen to the square.
