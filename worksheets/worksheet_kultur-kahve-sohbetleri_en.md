## 📌 SEVİYE A1: Coffee Time in the Garden

**Dil Bilgisi Odağı:** Present Simple, Present Continuous, Hospitality Words
> Mehmet Bey and Hasan drinking coffee in the garden told in simple present tense.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
In the afternoon, Mehmet Bey calls his neighbor Hasan to the garden. They sit at the wooden table in the garden and talk cheerfully.

Mehmet goes to the kitchen and brews fresh Turkish coffee in the cezve. The coffee has plenty of foam on top. He places cold water and two small Turkish delights beside the cups.

While sipping their coffee, the two old friends reminisce about old days. Drinking coffee together gives them great joy and tranquility.

## 📌 SEVİYE C1: Friendship Brewed in the Shade: The Memory Album of a Cup of Coffee

**Dil Bilgisi Odağı:** Natural Literary Register, Pure Narrative Flow, Subtle Characterization
> Mehmet Bey and Hasan Bey's forty-year friendship told in warm, natural literary Turkish without strained academic terms.

### 📖 Okuma Parçası (English)
In those tranquil hours when the late afternoon sun filtered through vine leaves onto the wooden table, the world in Mehmet Bey and Hasan Bey's garden seemed to slow down as if bashful of its own haste. With that sweet familiarity borne of forty years of acquaintance, as Mehmet Bey set the cezve on the stove, Hasan Bey would rest his tobacco tin on the table, neither needing to ponder where conversation should begin.

As the aroma of coffee, poured just before its froth could boil over, enveloped the garden, their talk concerned no grandiose speeches, but the neighbor's fig tree, letters from children, and unforgettable memories of youth. For Mehmet Bey and Hasan Bey, the forty-year gratitude of that cup was the purest gift of modest devotion, of an ancient friendship revering even each other's silence, and of the odyssey called life that turns beautiful when shared.

### 📚 Kilit Kelimeler (Key Vocabulary)
- **sweet familiarity**: sweet familiarity
- **modest devotion / unpretentious loyalty**: modest devotion / unpretentious loyalty
- **revering silence**: revering silence
- **the odyssey / adventure of life**: the odyssey / adventure of life
